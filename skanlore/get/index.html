What is Skanlore?

Every scan is a lesson hiding in plain sight

Skanlore is a camera-based vocabulary game for children aged 5–12. Kids point the camera at real objects around them, and Skanlore checks whether the object matches the quest's word.

Words stop being definitions on a page. Your child learns what cylindrical means by finding a cylinder, and discovers transparent by scanning a glass. Every correct find clears a dungeon room, earns XP and raises their Lexicon Rank.

Cylindrical→a water bottle
Transparent→a drinking glass
Flexible→a rubber band
Hollow→a cardboard tube

Three steps, and the whole world becomes the game board.

1

Questions parents ask

What age is Skanlore for?

Children aged 5–12. Hard Mode and parent-made quests let the difficulty grow as your child does.

Is it free?

Skanlore is free to download and includes a free quest every day. A Premium plan is available inside the app for families who want more daily scans; purchases sit behind the parent PIN.

Does it work on iPhone, iPad and Android?

Yes. Get it from the App Store for iPhone and iPad, or from Google Play for Android phones and tablets.

Does it need the internet?

Yes. Each scan is checked online so Skanlore can understand why an object fits a word. The camera image is used only for that check and is never stored.

Can siblings share one account?

Yes. Each child gets their own profile with separate progress, Word Tome and streaks, and siblings can compete on the family leaderboard.

Where can we play?

Anywhere with everyday objects: at home, at the beach, in the supermarket, while shopping or on a trip. Parents can create a quest for a specific place.
